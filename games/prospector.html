Prospector

Dig for gold in the old mine. Take all the time you want. Solo, or up to 8 prospectors in teams.

1LEFT PAD walks, hops and climbs. RIGHT PAD digs the block it points at.
2READ THE DIRT — black specks hint at ore. Warm round glints are gold, pale square glints are fool’s gold.
3GOLD IS HEAVY — you carry only 6. Bank it at the shack for cash, or load it into a rail cart.
4BUILD — spend cash at the shack on rails, carts and lamps. Rails run from a cart up to the surface; SEND hauls the load and banks it.
5LADDERS — tap LADDER to drop a run toward where you were heading.
6FALLS HURT — 3 cells or more costs hearts. Knocked out? You wake at the shack, your pack stays where you fell, carts stay safe. You can always haul yourself up, slowly.
Still testingProspector is a work in progress. Future updates may change the mine, prices, rules and saved bests, which can affect your progress. Solo games autosave on this device and you can Continue later.
