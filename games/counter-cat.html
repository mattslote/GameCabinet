Counter Cat

A sneaky cat is hiding behind your counter, reaching out to knock things off. Touch a falling item and drag it back onto the counter before it hits the floor. Watch out for fake-outs: touch an item that's only teetering and you'll fumble it. Broken items must be replaced in the shop. Between waves, buy glue to make things harder to knock over (the cat wears it down), or catnip to slow the cat.
