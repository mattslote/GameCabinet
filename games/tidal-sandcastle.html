Tidal Sandcastle

The tide is coming in. Press on the sand to grab a handful, drag it where you want it, and let go to drop it. Hands are quick but carry little. The shovel carries a lot more but moves slowly, and on the castle it pulls loose sand in and packs it so it lasts. Loose sand washes away fast. But the ocean always wins: the tide never turns, it only rises faster. How long can you hold it?
