Chip & Grain

A wood lathe. The blank spins on a bar, you shape it, then paint it to match the customer's order. They inspect it by hand and score it from 1% to 100%.

  1. Turn. Press a tool into the spinning wood. Wood only comes off, so go slowly.
  2. Sand. Tool marks cost you points. Sandpaper smooths them.
  3. Paint. Pick a brush and a color and touch it to the spinning piece. It paints a ring.

On a touch screen the tool lands on the surface under your finger. Drag down to push it in, drag up to back off.
